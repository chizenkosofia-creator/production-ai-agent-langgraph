# Refund Policy

Customers on the Starter plan can request a full refund within 14 days of the
first payment. Growth and Enterprise customers are refunded pro rata for unused
full months when they cancel mid-contract. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5-10 business days. Annual prepaid plans are not refundable
after day 30, but the account stays active until the end of the paid term.

Refund requests above 5,000 USD require approval from a finance manager.

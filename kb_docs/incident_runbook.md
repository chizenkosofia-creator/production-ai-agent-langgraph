# Incident Escalation Runbook

An incident is urgent when more than 20% of a customer's users are blocked or
when data loss is suspected. For urgent incidents: open a ticket with priority
`urgent`, page the on-call engineer through the #oncall Slack channel, and notify
the customer's account owner within 15 minutes.

Severity 2 incidents (degraded performance, workaround available) use priority
`high` and must receive a first response within 2 business hours. Every incident
needs a written postmortem within 5 business days of resolution.
